# Listing Templates for Calligraphy Digital Service

This directory contains ready-to-use templates for listing your calligraphy services on various online marketplaces.

## Contents

1. **etsy-listing-template.md** - Complete Etsy listing with description, pricing, and policies
2. **fiverr-gig-template.md** - Full Fiverr gig setup with packages and requirements

## Quick Start Guide

### For Etsy:
1. Open `etsy-listing-template.md`
2. Copy the relevant sections into your Etsy listing form
3. Upload your sample images (from `/samples` directory)
4. Adjust pricing based on your experience and market
5. Review and publish

### For Fiverr:
1. Open `fiverr-gig-template.md`
2. Follow the package structure (Basic, Standard, Premium)
3. Upload portfolio images showing different styles
4. Set up gig extras for additional revenue
5. Activate and promote

## Customization Tips

### Pricing Strategy:
- **Start Lower:** When beginning, price competitively to build reviews
- **Increase Gradually:** Raise prices as you gain ratings and experience
- **Value-Based:** Price based on complexity, not just word count
- **Market Research:** Check competitor pricing in your niche

### Photography Requirements:
- Use samples from the `/samples` directory
- Take high-quality photos with good lighting
- Show variety in your portfolio (different styles, colors)
- Include close-ups to show detail and craftsmanship
- Add mockups showing work in context (framed, on products)

### Writing Your Bio:
- Highlight your passion for calligraphy
- Mention years of experience (if applicable)
- Share what makes your style unique
- Include any relevant training or certifications
- Keep it friendly and approachable

## Success Metrics to Track

1. **Views:** How many people see your listing
2. **Conversion Rate:** Views to orders percentage
3. **Average Order Value:** Total revenue / number of orders
4. **Customer Satisfaction:** Reviews and ratings
5. **Repeat Customers:** Percentage who order again

## Optimization Tips

### Etsy SEO:
- Use all 13 tag slots with relevant keywords
- Include keywords naturally in title and description
- Update listings with new photos regularly
- Offer free shipping if possible (build into price)
- Enable Etsy Ads for initial visibility boost

### Fiverr Ranking:
- Respond to messages within 1 hour
- Deliver orders on time or early
- Encourage buyers to leave reviews
- Use all 5 gig tags effectively
- Create a gig video (improves visibility by 200%)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Using stock photos instead of your own work
2. ❌ Unclear pricing or hidden fees
3. ❌ Slow response times to inquiries
4. ❌ Over-promising and under-delivering
5. ❌ Ignoring negative feedback
6. ❌ Not updating portfolio with best work
7. ❌ Copying other sellers' descriptions word-for-word

## Legal Considerations

### Copyright:
- You own the copyright to your calligraphy artwork
- Specify usage rights in your terms (commercial vs personal)
- Keep original scans/files for your records

### Terms of Service:
- Clearly state turnaround times
- Define what constitutes a "revision"
- Explain your cancellation/refund policy
- Specify file formats and usage rights

### Client Content:
- You may display client work in your portfolio (with permission)
- Don't share client personal information
- Respect confidentiality for business projects

## Scaling Your Business

### When to Raise Prices:
- After receiving 10+ five-star reviews
- When you have more orders than you can handle
- After developing a unique or in-demand style
- When adding new skills or techniques

### Expanding Services:
- Digital files → Physical prints
- Single pieces → Bundle packages
- Custom work → Template collections
- One-time orders → Subscription boxes

### Building Your Brand:
- Create consistent branding across platforms
- Develop a signature style
- Build an email list of customers
- Consider creating your own website
- Expand to wholesale or licensing
