## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

1. **Create Your Samples**
   - Use the descriptions in `/samples` as inspiration
   - Hand-write 5-10 pieces showcasing different styles
   - Scan at 300 DPI minimum
   - Save as high-quality JPG or PNG files

2. **Set Up Your Listings**
   - Choose Etsy, Fiverr, or both
   - Copy relevant templates from `/templates`
   - Customize pricing based on your experience
   - Upload your sample images
   - Launch your gig/listing

3. **Promote on Social Media**
   - Use Instagram story templates from `/social-media`
   - Post before/after sequences of your work
   - Engage with potential customers
   - Link to your marketplace listings

## 📸 Portfolio Tips

1. **Lighting:** Natural light near a window, avoid harsh shadows
2. **Background:** Clean white or light neutral colors
3. **Composition:** Mix of full pieces and close-up details
4. **Variety:** Show different styles, colors, and types of work
5. **Context:** Include mockups showing work framed or in use

## 🎨 Style Options to Offer

- **Modern Calligraphy:** Contemporary, bouncy, artistic
- **Elegant Script:** Traditional, formal, sophisticated  
- **Whimsical:** Playful, decorative, fun
- **Custom:** Match client's reference or brand style

## 🛠️ Materials Needed

### Essential:
- Calligraphy pen or brush pen
- High-quality ink (black + 2-3 colors)
- Smooth paper (HP Premium32 or Rhodia)
- Scanner or high-quality camera (300 DPI minimum)

### Optional but Recommended:
- Lightbox for tracing
- Multiple nib sizes
- Gouache or watercolor for colored inks
- Metallic inks (gold, rose gold)
- Textured papers for variety
