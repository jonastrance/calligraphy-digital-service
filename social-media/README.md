# Social Media Templates for Calligraphy Business

This directory contains templates and guidelines for promoting your calligraphy services on social media platforms.

## Contents

1. **instagram-story-templates.md** - Before/after story templates for showcasing your work
2. **instagram-post-templates.md** - Feed post templates and content ideas
3. **content-calendar.md** - Sample posting schedule and planning guide

## Platform Overview

### Instagram
- **Primary platform** for visual showcase
- Use Stories for process/behind-the-scenes
- Use Feed for portfolio and finished work
- Use Reels for time-lapse and tutorials
- Use Highlights to organize content by category

### Pinterest
- Create pins linking to your Etsy/Fiverr listings
- Share calligraphy inspiration boards
- Create "how-to" content for reach

### TikTok (Optional)
- Short time-lapse videos of calligraphy process
- Satisfying ink flourish videos
- Quick tips and lettering tutorials

### Facebook
- Share to personal profile and create business page
- Join calligraphy and wedding planning groups
- Post finished work with detailed descriptions

## General Best Practices

1. **Consistency:** Post regularly (3-5x per week minimum)
2. **Quality:** Use high-resolution photos with good lighting
3. **Engagement:** Respond to comments and DMs promptly
4. **Hashtags:** Use relevant tags (see templates for suggestions)
5. **Call-to-Action:** Always include clear next steps for customers
6. **Brand Identity:** Maintain consistent colors, fonts, and style
7. **Authenticity:** Show your personality and process

## Content Pillars

1. **Portfolio Showcase** (40%) - Finished work, best pieces
2. **Process/BTS** (30%) - Work in progress, setup shots
3. **Educational** (15%) - Tips, tutorials, calligraphy facts
4. **Engagement** (10%) - Questions, polls, community interaction
5. **Promotional** (5%) - Direct selling posts, special offers

## Tools & Resources

- **Scheduling:** Later, Planoly, or Meta Business Suite
- **Design:** Canva, Adobe Express, Unfold
- **Photography:** Natural lighting + smartphone camera
- **Editing:** VSCO, Lightroom Mobile, Snapseed
- **Analytics:** Instagram Insights, Pinterest Analytics

---

*Remember: Quality over quantity. It's better to post less frequently with excellent content than daily with mediocre content.*
