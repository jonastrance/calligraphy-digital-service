# Instagram Story Templates for Before/After Calligraphy Samples

## Overview
These templates help you showcase your calligraphy process and finished work on Instagram Stories, building engagement and demonstrating your skill.

## Story Dimensions
- Size: 1080 x 1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio)
- Format: PNG or JPG
- File size: Under 8MB for optimal upload

## Posting Strategy & Best Practices

### Timing:
- Post "BEFORE" stories when you receive the order
- Post "AFTER" stories when completed (same day or next day)
- Create anticipation with the gap between posts

### Engagement Tactics:
- **Polls:** "Which style should I use? A) Modern B) Elegant"
- **Questions:** "What would you want written in calligraphy?"
- **Countdown:** "Finishing this in 2 hours! ⏰"
- **Quiz:** "Can you guess what this says?" (teaser of work)

### Hashtag Strategy (use in first comment):
```
#calligraphy #handlettering #moderncalligraphy 
#weddingcalligraphy #customcalligraphy #calligraphyart
#handwritten #calligraphylove #penmanship #calligraphyph
```

### Call-to-Action Variations:
- "DM me 'QUOTE' for pricing 💌"
- "Link in bio for instant booking 🔗"
- "Tag someone who needs this 👇"
- "Save this for later! 📌"
- "Available on Etsy - link in bio 🛍️"

### Story Highlights:
Create highlights for:
- 📦 "Orders" (Before/After sequences)
- 💰 "Pricing" (Package information)
- ⭐ "Reviews" (Customer testimonials)
- 🎨 "Portfolio" (Best work showcase)
- ❓ "FAQ" (Common questions answered)

## Design Tools & Resources

### Recommended Apps:
- **Canva:** Pre-sized Instagram story templates
- **Unfold:** Minimalist story templates
- **StoryArt:** Quick template application
- **Adobe Spark:** Custom branded templates
- **Over:** Text overlay and design elements

### Design Tips:
1. Keep text large and readable (mobile-first)
2. Use contrasting colors for visibility
3. Leave "safe zones" at top/bottom (250px margins recommended to avoid being cut off by Instagram UI)
4. Brand consistently (colors, fonts, logo placement)
5. Use high-quality photos (avoid blur and pixelation)
6. Add subtle animation (using Instagram's features)

### Photography Tips for Calligraphy:
- Natural lighting (near window)
- Clean, uncluttered background
- Straight overhead angle for flatlay
- Slight angle to show texture and dimension
- Multiple shots (wide, medium, close-up)
- Include workspace for "behind the scenes"
